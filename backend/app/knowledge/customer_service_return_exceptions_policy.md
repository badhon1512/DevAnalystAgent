# Customer Service Return Exceptions Policy

Document owner: Customer Operations
Department: Customer Operations
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Purpose

This policy defines how customer service teams should handle return exceptions, refund escalation, proof-of-purchase gaps, partial refunds, exchange alternatives, and customer communications. It extends the Return and Refund Policy 2026 and should be used when a return request does not fit the standard rules cleanly.

The goal is to keep customer decisions consistent, auditable, and fair while protecting inventory accuracy and margin. Exceptions should be rare, documented, and tied to clear business reasons.

## Standard Decision Order

Customer service should evaluate return cases in this order:

- Verify the original sale or order record.
- Confirm the request is within the standard return window or qualifies for an exception.
- Identify the return reason.
- Confirm the product category and any category-specific restrictions.
- Confirm item condition after receipt or based on customer evidence.
- Select refund, exchange, repair, store credit, or decline.
- Record the decision reason and approval when required.

If live order, product, inventory, or return data is unavailable, the team should avoid promising a final outcome. The correct response is to explain what information is missing and what must be verified.

## Proof of Purchase Exceptions

Proof of purchase is normally required. Acceptable proof includes order number, receipt number, invoice number, account purchase history, payment confirmation, marketplace order reference, or customer service case reference.

When proof of purchase is missing, customer service may still consider store credit if the product is identifiable, the item appears to be sold by the company, the customer history is low risk, and the item is not regulated, personalized, or final sale. Refund to original payment method is not allowed without verified sale evidence.

Manager approval is required when proof is missing and the estimated item value is above 75 EUR. Returns without proof above 250 EUR should normally be declined unless a director-level customer recovery exception is approved.

## Return windows
The standard return window is 30 days.
## Late Return Exceptions

Returns outside the standard window may be approved only when a documented exception applies. Valid reasons include carrier delay, incorrect item shipped, defective item, delayed delivery evidence, warranty-backed failure, documented customer service error, or approved goodwill recovery.

Goodwill late returns should consider customer value, return history, item condition, resale potential, and margin impact. Goodwill should normally be store credit rather than cash refund or original payment refund.

Late returns more than 15 days beyond the standard window require team lead approval. Late returns more than 45 days beyond the standard window require operations manager approval unless covered by warranty or legal obligation.

## Partial Refund Rules

Partial refunds may be used when the customer keeps the item, when accessories are missing, when packaging damage reduces resale value, when a minor defect does not prevent use, or when shipping/service recovery is appropriate.

Suggested partial refund bands:

- 5 to 10 percent for minor cosmetic packaging damage.
- 10 to 20 percent for missing non-critical accessories.
- 15 to 30 percent for opened-good items with reduced resale value.
- 20 to 50 percent for accepted damaged items where customer misuse is possible.

Partial refunds above 50 percent should be treated like a full refund approval and require the same approval level.

## Exchange Preference

When the customer wants a usable product and inventory is available, exchange should be offered before refund for defective, damaged-on-arrival, incorrect-item, and fulfillment-error cases.

Customer service must not promise an exchange until inventory availability is checked. If stock is below reorder point or available quantity is below five units, the team should communicate that replacement timing depends on warehouse confirmation or replenishment timing.

If the replacement item is unavailable, acceptable alternatives are refund, store credit, waitlist for replenishment, or substitute SKU when approved by the customer.

## High-Risk Return Patterns

The following patterns require fraud or team lead review before refund:

- Repeated missing-item claims.
- Frequent high-value returns.
- Multiple accounts using the same address or payment method.
- Serial number mismatch.
- Return package contains unrelated item.
- Customer repeatedly requests refund before inspection.
- High-value item returned with damaged or altered packaging.

High-risk review does not automatically mean the return is declined. It means refund should wait until sale verification, item inspection, and decision documentation are complete.

## Communication Standards

Customer communication should be specific but not overly legalistic. Staff should explain what rule applies, what evidence is needed, and what outcome is available.

When declining a return, the message should include the primary reason, such as outside return window, final-sale category, missing proof of purchase, failed inspection, or fraud review. Do not blame warehouse, suppliers, carriers, or individual employees.

When approving an exception, the note should state that the decision is an exception and should not be described as a standard policy entitlement.

## Required Notes

Every exception must include:

- Original sale or order reference when available.
- Return reason.
- Product condition or expected inspection requirement.
- Selected resolution.
- Approval owner when approval is required.
- Customer communication summary.
- Inventory disposition when known.

Missing notes should be treated as an audit issue. A return exception without a note may be reversed or escalated during review.

## Agent Guidance

When answering return exception questions, the assistant should cite this policy together with the Return and Refund Policy 2026. For specific customer, product, stock, sales, or return-rate questions, the assistant should use SQL tools for live data and this document for policy rules. The assistant should distinguish a standard entitlement from an approved exception.
